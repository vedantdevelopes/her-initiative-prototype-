Who we are

Not an
organization.
A community.

HER Initiative started as a simple idea between a few people who felt like something was missing — a space where women could actually speak without performing, where conversations didn't need to be polished or on-brand.

We're youth-led. We're messy sometimes. We don't have all the answers. But we do have each other, and we think that counts for a lot.

Through conversations, events, interactive experiences, and content — we're building something that feels less like a platform and more like a place you'd actually want to come back to.

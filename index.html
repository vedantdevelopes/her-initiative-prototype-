Who we are

We are HER
Initiative.

Her.

HER Initiative is a youth-led platform built to create more space for women's voices, stories, ideas, and experiences — through conversations, community events, interactive experiences, and content.

We want it to feel less like a formal organisation and more like a real community where people connect, share, learn, and grow together.

"Because every woman's voice deserves to be heard."

✦

Real conversations

Not panels. Not presentations. Just women talking to each other with honesty and care.

◎

Community, not audience

⬡

Safe and intentional

What's shared in the room stays in the room. We hold every voice with care.

Youth-led, always

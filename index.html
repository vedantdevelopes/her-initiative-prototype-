Who we are

We are HER Initiative.

A youth-led platform creating space for women's voices, stories, ideas, and experiences — through conversations, community events, and interactive experiences. Less like a formal organisation, more like a real community.

HER Initiative started with a simple belief: that women's voices deserve more than a seat at the table — they deserve the whole room. After Hours is our most intimate format yet. A small gathering, a held space, and the freedom to just be.

We're not a conference. We're not a networking event. We're a community that connects, shares, learns, and grows — and After Hours is the night we get to feel that most.

What to expect.
